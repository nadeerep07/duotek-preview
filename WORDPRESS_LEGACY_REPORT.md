# Duotek Legacy WordPress Website Report

Prepared: 14 October 2026  
Archive reviewed: `public_html/` (legacy WordPress installation)

## Purpose

This report preserves the useful business, content, technical, and media information recovered from Duotek's previous WordPress website before the legacy `public_html` directory was removed from this project.

The report is intended as a reference for future website copy, service pages, SEO planning, company profiles, and content verification. It does not contain WordPress/database credentials.

## Important recovery limitation

The archive contained WordPress application files, themes, plugins, generated Elementor CSS, and uploaded media, but it did **not** contain a WordPress SQL/database export.

WordPress stores page copy, post copy, navigation menus, settings, user records, Elementor layouts, and many plugin settings in its database. The archived `wp-config.php` referenced a remote database, but database credentials and other secrets were deliberately excluded from this report.

Consequently, the findings below combine:

- Evidence present in the legacy filesystem and media library.
- Generated Elementor page CSS and historical asset names.
- Publicly indexed copies of the former Duotek pages retrieved during the review.

Claims copied from the old website should be treated as historical company claims—not independently verified facts—unless supported by company records.

## Recommended company positioning

Duotek should be presented primarily as an **IT and ELV infrastructure company**, with software engineering as an established complementary practice.

Recommended positioning statement:

> Established in Dubai in 2015, Duotek Information Technologies LLC delivers IT and ELV infrastructure, managed technical support, networking, communications, security, and smart automation across the UAE. Its software practice extends that foundation through custom web platforms, mobile applications, cloud services, and workflow automation.

This positioning is consistent with both the legacy website and the current service direction. It avoids presenting Duotek as only an ELV contractor or overstating it as a software-only agency.

- The 2015 establishment date is consistent with the site's own historical narrative and can support “Established in 2015” or “10+ years” wording.

- Avoid “leading,” “number one,” “most trusted,” or similar claims unless independently supportable.

## Claims that require verification before reuse

The legacy site made or implied the following claims:

- More than 1,000 clients.
- Certified and experienced personnel.
- Leading IT company in Dubai.
- High-level security and wide regional coverage.
- Guaranteed top-quality solutions.

Recommended treatment:

- **Safe with basic records:** established in Dubai in 2015; Business Village office address; listed contact details; broad service capabilities that are still actively delivered.
- **Use with supporting documents:** certifications, SIRA-related compliance, vendor-partner status, Fluke certification, SLAs, response times, and 24/7 support.
- **Avoid until proven:** precise client totals, market-leadership claims, guaranteed results, universal zero downtime, and blanket compliance statements.

## Content weaknesses from the legacy site

The former site contained several patterns that should not be carried forward:

- Generic claims such as “leading,” “perfect,” and “best.”
- Template/placeholder copy, including visible lorem ipsum in the news and newsletter sections.
- Grammar and capitalization inconsistencies.
- Keyword-heavy service wording.
- Stock people presented near team/testimonial sections.
- Product references without clear partnership qualifications.
- Conflicting counters and milestones.

The current editorial site is visually and structurally stronger. Legacy content should be used as background evidence, not copied verbatim.

## Privacy and legal-document note

The old site's navigation referenced a Privacy Statement and Terms of Service, but complete legal text was not recovered from the filesystem.

Because the current website collects names, business email addresses, phone numbers, service selections, and project details through Web3Forms, its privacy notice should identify:

- Duotek as the data controller.
- The data collected and purpose of collection.
- Web3Forms and relevant hosting/analytics providers as processors or recipients where applicable.
- Retention practices.
- Contact and data-subject request procedures.
- Cross-border processing where applicable.
- Cookie and analytics use.

Final legal documents should be reviewed by a UAE-qualified lawyer or compliance consultant against the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and the company's actual operating practices. Generic template text should not be published without customization.
