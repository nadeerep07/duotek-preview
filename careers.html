Recruitment Workflow

Our 4-Stage Recruitment Process

We respect every applicant's time and effort. Here is what you can expect when applying to Duotek.

Stage 01

Application & CV Review

Our hiring team reviews your submitted CV, certifications, and portfolio against current project demands and technical requirements.

Stage 02

Initial Communication

Shortlisted candidates are contacted for a preliminary discussion regarding background, career aspirations, salary expectations, and availability.

Stage 03

Interview & Assessment

A comprehensive interview with technical leads and management, including a practical or role-related technical assessment where appropriate.

Stage 04

Selection & Offer

Final selection, formal offer discussions, agreement on start date, onboarding schedule, and employment documentation under UAE labor regulations.

Note: Submitting an application does not guarantee an interview or employment. Due to the high volume of applications, only shortlisted candidates may be contacted by our HR team.

Equal Opportunity & Professional Conduct

Duotek welcomes applications from qualified and motivated candidates regardless of background. Recruitment decisions are based strictly on relevant skills, experience, qualifications, role requirements, and applicable law.
