Who We Are

A Dubai IT company connecting infrastructure, security, and software.

Founded in Dubai in 2015, Duotek began with a practical goal: help UAE businesses run dependable technology environments. Our core work covers IT support, structured cabling, networking, servers, IP telephony, CCTV, access control, and smart building systems.

Our software engineering practice works alongside that infrastructure capability. We design web platforms, mobile applications, cloud APIs, integrations, and workflow automations that connect with the systems, teams, and physical spaces our clients already use.

From site assessment and installation to software delivery, documentation, maintenance, and ongoing support, each engagement is scoped around the client's operational requirements rather than a one-size-fits-all package.

Our Methodology

A disciplined 6-step project approach.

A clear delivery path that keeps requirements, responsibilities, testing, and handover visible from the start.

Step 01

Consultation & Assessment

Understanding client requirements, site blueprints, infrastructure capacity, and technical objectives.

Step 02

Planning & Design

Architecting a reliable, scalable technical solution based on scope, site conditions, and budget.

Step 03

Supply & Installation

Coordinating equipment, structured cabling, rack dressing, and professional on-site installation.

Step 04

Configuration & Testing

Configuring routers, PBX, access permissions, NVRs, and testing operation before handover.

Step 05

Documentation & Handover

Providing labeling schemes, configuration documentation, user training, and test verification reports.

Step 06

Maintenance & Support

Providing ongoing technical support, preventive visits, and fast response via tailored AMC agreements.
